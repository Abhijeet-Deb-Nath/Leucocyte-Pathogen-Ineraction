# Future Prospect

This folder is the planning area for research directions that are intentionally outside the scope of the current paper.

The current paper should remain frozen around:

- the existing partially observable immune-control benchmark
- the heuristic baseline
- the hierarchical `BC + DAgger` learned mainline
- the negative `BC-only` and `BC + DAgger + RL` ablations
- the corrected robustness suite

Anything in this folder is a later branch, not a silent extension of the current manuscript.

## Core Principle

Do not mix future branches into the current paper unless the paper is deliberately re-scoped from scratch.

The purpose of this folder is to keep future work explicit, ranked, and technically honest.
